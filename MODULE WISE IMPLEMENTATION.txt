Module 1: User Authentication and Authorization

Purpose:
User identity management and role separation.

Implementation:

User registration and login

Role-based access (Admin, Customer)

Token-based authentication

Module 2: Product and Inventory Management

Purpose:
Management of core business data.

Implementation:

Add, update, and delete products

Manage stock quantity

Identify low-stock products

Product search and filtering

Key Rules:

Stock quantity must never be negative

Product price stored once

Module 3: Cart Management

Purpose:
Temporary staging of customer orders.

Implementation:

Add product to cart

Update cart quantity

Remove product from cart

View cart summary

Key Rules:

Cart and order are separate entities

Stock is not reduced at cart stage

Module 4: Order Processing

Purpose:
Execution of business-critical order workflow.

Implementation:

Place order

Validate stock availability

Calculate total amount

Create order and order items

Update inventory atomically

Order Lifecycle:

Placed → Shipped → Delivered

Module 5: Admin Order Management

Purpose:
Administrative control over order handling.

Implementation:

View all orders

Update order status

Enforce valid order state transitions

Module 6: Reports and Analytics

Purpose:
Business monitoring and decision support.

Implementation:

Daily and monthly sales reports

Low-stock inventory reports

Orders grouped by status
